App Cost Calculator FAQ

Clear answers to the most common questions about how Forfis AI Calculator
works, its accuracy, and what you get from it.

How accurate are the cost estimates?

Our estimates are based on Tier-1 market benchmarks, real project data, and AI-driven scope modeling. Results are provided as realistic ranges rather than optimistic averages.

Can I customize or change features after generating the plan?

Absolutely. The generated plan is a starting point — features, scope, and assumptions can be refined with our product and technical teams.

How secure is the data I submit?

Your project details are never stored or shared unless you choose to contact our team. And remember, execution matters more than the idea itself — your app's success depends on marketing, team, and business model, not just the concept. The calculator is fully automated.

How fast do I get the results?

A lean mobile MVP usually takes 9–11 weeks from wireframes to app store launch. A mid-level app with payments or chat features needs about 16–22 weeks, while advanced AI or real-time apps require 24–30 weeks.

What AI technology is behind the cost estimation process?

The calculator runs on and GPT-4o and uses DALLE-3 for quick design estimates. These models are trained on data from 10 000+ real projects, so numbers match actual market rates and complexity.

How much does it cost to build an AI-powered application?

The cost varies significantly depending on AI complexity, data requirements, integrations, and scalability. AI-powered applications typically require higher upfront investment than standard apps due to model development, infrastructure, and compliance needs.

Which types of apps generate the highest revenue?

Apps with recurring revenue models typically perform best, including SaaS platforms, fintech solutions, marketplaces, healthcare products, and AI-enabled business tools.

Is mobile app development still profitable in 2025?

Yes. Mobile apps remain highly profitable when built around clear market demand, strong product differentiation, and sustainable monetization models. Success increasingly depends on execution quality rather than platform choice alone.

How much does it cost to maintain and operate an app long-term?

Long-term maintenance costs depend on product complexity, infrastructure usage, security requirements, third-party services, and ongoing feature development. Operating an app typically includes hosting, monitoring, updates, bug fixes, and continuous improvements to support growth and user demand.

Does it include design and AI?

Yes. The estimate includes product design (UX/UI), technical architecture, and AI-related components where applicable, based on your selected features and industry.

Can I use this for fundraising?

Yes. The generated estimate and technical scope are often used by founders as a high-level validation tool for investor discussions. While it’s not a replacement for full due diligence, it helps communicate feasibility, scope, and budget assumptions clearly.

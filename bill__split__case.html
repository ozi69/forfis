The challenge we faced

The Problem

Before launching the product, the business faced typical market constraints:

MANUAL CALCULATIONS

Manual expense splitting caused errors and unnecessary conflicts.

LARGE GROUP COMPLEXITY

Most apps struggled with 5–10+ participants and uneven shares.

MULTI-CURRENCY ISSUES

Multi-currency splits were inconvenient, slow, or inaccurate.

HIGH-FRICTION UX

Too many steps reduced usage frequency and repeat behavior.

The turning point

The Insight

In shared payment situations, even minor complexity creates hesitation, especially in social contexts where speed and clarity matter.

Every additional step, input, or confirmation increases friction and raises the likelihood of mistakes, awkwardness, or abandoned splits.

When bill sharing becomes instant and predictable, usage frequency increases naturally and the product integrates into everyday group behavior.

The moves that unlocked growth

The Breakthrough Solutions

Based on research, several deliberate decisions were made:

ONE-TAP SPLITTING

Split bills instantly with minimal input.

GROUP-FIRST FLOW

Built for large groups and uneven shares by default.

REAL-TIME CURRENCY

Accurate multi-currency splits with live rates.

AUTO BALANCE UPDATES

Debts rebalance automatically as payments happen.

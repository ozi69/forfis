The challenge we faced

The Problem

Before launching the product, the business faced typical market constraints:

Fragmented Aviation Data

Flight, fleet, and pricing data were scattered across disconnected systems.

Slow Booking Process

Confirming a private flight often took days instead of minutes.

Trust Barrier

High-value transactions required absolute transparency and platform reliability.

Complex User Roles

Clients, operators, and brokers operated in separate systems without a unified interface.

The turning point

The Insight

From the very beginning, Rocket Jet was approached as a long-term aviation platform rather than a one-off digital product. Through in-depth discovery, we immersed ourselves in the private aviation market by speaking directly with operators and charter clients, analyzing global competitors, and mapping user journeys for clients, operators, and internal teams. This process allowed us to validate monetization and scaling models early on and ensure that the platform’s foundation was built for sustainable growth, operational efficiency, and long-term market relevance.

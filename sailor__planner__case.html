Executive Summary

Sailor Planner is a cross-platform planning and tracking solution built for maritime professionals operating in offshore and low-connectivity environments. The product combines offline-first architecture, role-based workflows, and navigation-focused UX to support safe and accurate voyage planning. It scales from individual sailors to organized crews and training institutions.

The challenge we faced

The Problem

Before launching the product, the business faced typical market constraints:

Unstable Connectivity

Maritime crews often operate without internet access, rendering standard planners ineffective.

High Cost of Mistakes

Planning errors had a direct impact on crew safety and operational outcomes.

Multi-Role Complexity

Different crew roles required distinct workflows and permission levels.

Long-Duration Events

Multi-day and multi-week sea voyages did not fit traditional calendar tools.

The turning point

The Insight

The core challenge was to design a system that operates reliably using an offline-first approach,adapts the interface and data presentation based on user roles and scales from individual sailors to organized teams and training centers.

The moves that unlocked growth

The Breakthrough Solutions

Based on research, several deliberate decisions were made:

Offline-First Architecture

The application remains fully functional without internet connectivity, ensuring reliability at sea.

Role-Adaptive Workflows

Interfaces and data views automatically adjust based on user roles onboard.

Voyage-Centric Planning

Multi-day sea journeys are visualized through clear timelines designed for maritime use.

Safety-Oriented UX

Critical data is surfaced clearly to minimize errors under physical and cognitive load

Making it real

How It Was Built

Offline-First Data Storage

All critical voyage data is stored locally and synced when connectivity is available.

Route Visualization Engine

Sea routes are displayed clearly with waypoints and timelines.

Automatic Activity Logging

Key events are recorded with minimal user input.

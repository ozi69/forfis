AI-Powered Water Logistics at Scale

Executive Summary

Water delivery is a simple product with a complex unit economy. Orders are repetitive, margins are thin, and logistics are operationally heavy.

Vodacom was created to solve one core problem: to make repeat orders as fast and cost-efficient as possible for the business.

We built a mobile platform where an AI assistant handles the majority of repeat orders, while the application remains a reliable fallback for all other scenarios.

The challenge we faced

The Problem

Before launching the product, the business faced typical market constraints:

High Cost of Reorders

Repeat orders were processed manually, making them expensive and difficult to scale.

Logistics Errors

Manual data entry led to frequent mistakes in water quantities and delivery execution.

Operator Dependency

The business relied heavily on call-center operators, increasing costs as demand grew.

Unpredictable Demand

Lack of demand forecasting made route planning and capacity management inefficient.

The turning point

The Insight

We discovered a simple but critical fact: more than 70 percent of orders are repeated with little to no changes.

Users don’t need to “choose.” They need to “repeat.” The problem wasn’t the interface — it was that the business was manually servicing the same scenario over and over again.

The moves that unlocked growth

The Breakthrough Solutions

AI-Powered Reorders

Recurring orders are handled automatically by AI, eliminating manual processing and reducing operational costs

Demand Forecasting Engine

Predictive analytics enable accurate planning of routes, inventory, and delivery schedules

Smart Logistics Optimization

Delivery routes and workloads are dynamically optimized based on real consumption patterns

Operator Load Reduction

Most repeat scenarios no longer require human involvement, allowing the business to scale without growing headcount
